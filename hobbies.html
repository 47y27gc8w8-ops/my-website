Outside Code

Handball and guitar keep the balance.

One is fast, loud, and physical. The other is calm, repetitive, and creative. Both help me practice focus in a different way.

Team Sport

Fast decisions, trust, and pressure.

Handball teaches me to read a situation quickly, react with control, and still trust the people around me. I like the pace because it forces me to stay awake and make decisions without overthinking everything.

Music

A slower way to practice creativity.

Guitar has the same satisfying loop as programming: learn a pattern, repeat it, clean it up, and slowly turn rough noise into something that feels good. It keeps the creative side active without needing a screen.
